Meet Karon

Karon Fayerweather is a women’s empowerment speaker, coach, two-time bestselling author, and the lyricist and creative visionary behind the album Boldly Becoming Her. Drawing on her own experience of rebuilding in midlife, she helps women—especially Black women—reclaim who they are, make deliberate choices, and create room for the life they want now through her Her Bold Second Act™ framework. Her album carries that message into music, giving voice to the tenderness, courage, and uncertainty of healing and beginning again. With honesty, warmth, and conviction, she reminds women that their next chapter deserves more than their endurance—it deserves their presence.
