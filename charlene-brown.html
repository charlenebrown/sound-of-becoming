Meet Charlene

Charlene Brown, known as da Tech Conjurer™, is a Songwriter and Digital Music Creator and a Creative Consultant with Brand Misfit™ Media. She brings more than 20 years across tech, brand strategy, and digital communications, along with experience as a Creative Director and Project Manager, to everything she makes. That means a song goes from concept to lyrics to sound to finished release with intention at every step. Brooklyn-rooted and Miami-raised by Caribbean parents, she brings culture, humor, and straight talk to every room. Her work turns brand philosophy into songs people feel. Her rule: clarity creates better choices, so lead with tech and never be led by it.

IN HER OWN WORDS

Why this album.
Why this moment.

Music has always been a consistent thread in my life. Over the years, I’ve connected it with ideas that may seem off the beaten path but speak to experiences so many of us share. I had stories, insights, and hopes I wanted to express that didn’t fit naturally into my training, client work, or the books I’ve written.

This project gave those thoughts another way to be heard. By bringing my words together with rhythm and feeling, I could share my experiences and hopes through the connection only music can create—one that reaches our core and speaks to our spirit. This album is another way for me to tell my story and connect with the people who hear a piece of their own lives in it.
