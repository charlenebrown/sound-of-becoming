Meet Shirley

Coach Shirley Ann Felder is a motivational speaker, life coach, comedian, bestselling co-author, and songwriter behind Ordinary Greatness. Known as the “Trash Talk Queen,” she draws on more than three decades of entrepreneurship and lived experience to help women release what weighs them down, recycle their lessons, reclaim their power, and rise. Her songs put her words and stories to music, sharing messages of resilience, self-worth, and becoming. With warmth, humor, and truth, she reminds women that their hardest chapters do not get the final word.
