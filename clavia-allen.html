THE WOMAN BEHIND THE WORDS

Her story.

Meet Clavia

Born in Trinidad and raised in Brooklyn, Clavia Allen now resides in Central Florida. Through her journey, Clavia has grown to know her worth, learning to let go, release what no longer serves her, and embrace each day as a new opportunity. As a servant leader of Chosen31 Essentials, Clavia embodies the mission of empowerment, healing, and growth. She leads with compassion, integrity, and vision, prioritizing the needs of her team and community while fostering an environment of support, transformation, and purpose. Her life and leadership reflect a commitment to helping others recognize their worth, walk boldly in their purpose, and understand that every new day offers another opportunity to become who they were chosen to be.

IN HER OWN WORDS

Why this album.
Why this moment.

The Chosen31 album is more than a musical project to me; it is my story in a nutshell, reflecting my roots, my growth, and the hand of God throughout my journey. I have been writing songs since I was young, primarily worship songs and songs of adoration to the Lord, so when this project was presented to me, I was excited to see my story come to life in a way beyond my own voice. Although I may not be the one singing, you can experience me in every part of this project, from where I was born and raised to the experiences that have shaped the woman I am today. Chosen31 is the beginning of something bigger than me, a testament to purpose, identity, and the beautiful ways God can use our stories to inspire others. My prayer is that through this project, people will see pieces of their own journeys, recognize that their stories matter, and above all, give God all the glory!
