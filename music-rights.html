Written permission comes first

No reproduction, redistribution, re-uploading, sampling, remixing, adaptation or commercial use of protected music or materials is authorized by this website. Obtain explicit prior written permission from the relevant woman or women and any other necessary rights holder(s) before proceeding, unless your specific use is already authorized by a valid license or applicable law.

Commercial projects

Permission is required where applicable for use in advertisements, sponsored or monetized posts, promotional videos, films, podcasts, paid programs, products, client projects, brand campaigns and other business or revenue-generating uses. A short excerpt, a purchase, a stream, event attendance or a download does not by itself grant those rights. Giving credit does not replace permission.

A song and its recording can involve different rights

The underlying song—including its music and lyrics—and a particular sound recording are separate works. Your project may need clearance for both, and permission from more than one party. Permission must cover the specific recording, purpose, platforms, territory and duration of your use. Learn more from the U.S. Copyright Office.

How to request permission

Start with the songwriter’s contact link on her profile. Include the song title, the recording or version, the excerpt and length, a description of your project, the intended platforms and audience, whether it is paid or monetized, the territory, the duration of use and the proposed release date. For help identifying the right contact, use Bklyn Custom Designs®. The organizer can help route an inquiry; it is not an automatic licensing agent for every work. Wait for the necessary written authorization before using the material. No response is not consent.
